Screening limitations

Not proof of authenticity

VoiceGuard estimates whether a recording resembles examples learned by one audio-classification model. The result is not a forensic conclusion, identity check, or guarantee.

Known failure conditions

  • AI generators, languages, voices, and accents absent from training data.
  • Audio replayed through a speaker and captured by another microphone.
  • Phone-call codecs, messaging-app compression, noise suppression, echo, music, and background noise.
  • Very short, quiet, clipped, edited, or non-speech samples.

Live detection is experimental

A low live score does not prove that a speaker is human. Re-recording can hide synthetic artifacts. Whenever possible, analyze the original audio file and verify suspicious communication through an independent channel.

Human review

Never deny access, accuse a person, publish a claim, or make legal, employment, financial, or safety decisions from one VoiceGuard result.
