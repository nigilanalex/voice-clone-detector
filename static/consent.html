Responsible-use notice

Recording and analysis consent

Only analyze speech when you have a lawful reason and the permission required where you live.

Before continuing

  • Tell participants when audio is being recorded or analyzed when required.
  • Do not use VoiceGuard for covert surveillance, harassment, or unauthorized biometric profiling.
  • Do not upload confidential, medical, legal, workplace, or children’s recordings without appropriate authority.
  • Do not treat one automated result as an accusation or final decision.

Live monitoring

Live microphone monitoring is experimental. It may capture nearby people and environmental sounds. Use it only with informed permission and keep the application visible while it is running.

Checking the consent box confirms that you accept responsibility for having permission to submit or monitor the audio.
