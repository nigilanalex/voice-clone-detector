Why a second device?

Android and iOS prevent ordinary websites and apps from intercepting cellular-call audio. Direct analysis is possible only when audio belongs to an app's own VoIP call.

Inside the detector

How the screening works

  1. 01
    Prepare

    Audio is converted to mono 16 kHz speech for consistent analysis.

  2. 02
    Inspect

    A Wav2Vec2 classifier checks several windows for learned synthetic patterns.

  3. 03
    Assess

    Window scores and audio quality become a clear, probabilistic risk result.
